TPE Floor Liners for Toyota Hilux Vigo Dual Cab

$18-20/set

Five-dimensional TPE floor mat moulded for the Toyota Hilux Vigo in dual cab form and left-hand drive, produced on tool W-46. The liner follows the footwell of the cab closely and the raised edge contains mud, water and gravel brought in from site work. Sets come in three or five pieces. TPE is odourless and plasticiser-free, halogen-free and recyclable, and does not harden in cold weather. Cleaning is simply a lift-out and a rinse. Buyers should confirm the cab configuration, drive side and model year. One set minimum, FOB pricing on request.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I confirm this fits my car?
This listing is for the Toyota Hilux Vigo Dual Cab. Send the year, body type and drive side; we check the tooling and confirm before production.
What is it made of?
TPE, a thermoplastic elastomer. It contains no plasticiser, so it does not give off the chemical smell PVC mats develop in a hot cabin, and it stays flexible in cold weather.
How do I clean it?
Shake out loose dirt, then hose it down or wipe with a damp cloth. Mild detergent is enough - no solvent and no machine washing.

Yes, OEM and ODM branding is available. Send your artwork, colour and packaging requirements with the enquiry.
