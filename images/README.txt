Images for tpematsfactory.com
====================

Required naming (referenced by products_data.py):
  images/<product-slug>.jpg          product main image, 816x816 recommended
  images/vehicles/<make>-<model>.jpg vehicle page image, 1200x800 recommended

Rules for this site:
  * factory-owned or own-product photos only - never competitor or branded images
  * no third-party brand marks, no OEM logos, no model codes from other companies
  * no watermarks, no supplier company names
  * one product = one image, JPG, under 200 KB, square or 4:3
